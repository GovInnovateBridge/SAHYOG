Screen a draft challenge

Paste a challenge's title, problem statement, and KPIs. The filter flags brand names, disproportionate eligibility bars, and non-outcome-based specs that would restrict fair competition — then suggests an outcome-based rewrite for each.

This is a standalone test view — in production this runs automatically before a challenge is published.

Built for the Government of Maharashtra — Department of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation (MSInS), under Problem Statement 26136.
